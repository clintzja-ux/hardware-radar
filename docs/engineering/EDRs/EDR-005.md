EDR-005

Title

Sentinel Engine Frozen After v1.0 Certification

Decision

After successful certification:

Engine behavior changes require a new architectural review.
New product categories should extend the engine through rule registration rather than modifying engine components.
Routine development shifts to schemas, rule sets, validators, and data—not engine redesign.

Reason

Protect the stability of the platform while allowing unlimited growth through extension.

EDR-005 (Approved)

Title

Sentinel Engine Frozen After v1.0 Certification

Decision

The Sentinel engine is certified as version 1.0 and enters a stability phase.

Future development should prioritize extending the platform through:

Schemas
Rule Sets
Validator functions
Knowledge modules

Changes to the Sentinel engine itself should be reserved for architectural improvements that benefit every product domain and should undergo formal architectural review.

Rationale

A stable, reusable engine allows the Hardware Intelligence Platform to grow by adding knowledge rather than rebuilding infrastructure.
