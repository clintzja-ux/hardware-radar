EDR-010
Forge is the exclusive authoring environment.
Decision

All Atlas and Mercury records shall originate through Forge or through future Forge-compatible ingestion pipelines.

Subsystems shall not directly author canonical records.

Rationale

This preserves:

validation
consistency
traceability
deterministic publication
Consequences

Atlas becomes read-only outside Forge.

Mercury observations follow the same publication path.

Sentinel validates before publication.

Hardware Radar never edits canonical records.
