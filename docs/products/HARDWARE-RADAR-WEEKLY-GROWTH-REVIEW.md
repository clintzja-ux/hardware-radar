# Hardware Radar Weekly Growth Review

**Status:** ACCEPTED
**Owner:** Mirabelle Labs
**Scope:** Audience and authority operating practice

## Governing loop

> **MEASURE → LEARN → EXPAND**

Hardware Radar should increasingly use observed audience behavior to decide which content to deepen, products or retailer destinations to add, tools to improve, hardware category to explore, and capabilities not to build yet. Evidence informs prioritization; it never overrides Hardware Radar's authority, truth, privacy, commercial-independence, provenance, or fail-closed doctrines.

This is a lightweight weekly review, not a dashboard requirement, publishing quota, automated decision system, or new analytics backend. Exactly one or a small number of evidence-supported priorities should normally emerge.

## Evidence reviewed

### Search Console — discovery and search opportunity

Review available impressions, clicks, click-through rate, queries, landing pages, average position, and relevant indexing or coverage issues. Use them to understand how people discover Hardware Radar and where search demand may be emerging.

### GA4 — onsite behavior, engagement, and referral action

Review users or sessions as appropriate, landing pages, page views, engagement, Guides, RAM catalog, product pages, comparison usage, traffic sources, and outbound retailer clicks. GA4 remains the near-term operational analytics owner.

### Retailer referrals — commercial-intent signal

Initially use GA4 outbound-click evidence to review total retailer clicks, receiving domains, originating Hardware Radar pages, products involved, and change over time. A click is a referral or interest signal—not a customer, buyer, purchase, order, sale, conversion, or revenue.

Safe reporting says: “Hardware Radar recorded X outbound retailer clicks during the period.” Aggregate referral evidence may later support affiliate applications or reapplications, retailer or sponsorship discussions, publisher credibility, and assessment of commercial value, but only within those truthful semantics.

### Social distribution — useful reach and discussion

Review official-channel posts, reach or impressions where available, meaningful engagement, profile visits, measurable site clicks, and topics producing useful discussion. Follower count alone is not success.

Initial guidance is approximately 70% useful hardware knowledge, 20% Hardware Radar philosophy/product development, and 10% direct promotion. During establishment, roughly one or two useful posts per day, selective following, normal human participation, and substantive factual replies may be appropriate. These are flexible launch guidelines, not quotas or permanent requirements. Avoid engagement bait, spam, repetitive links, fake urgency, unsupported Cheapest claims, invented testing, affiliate-biased coverage, mass following/unfollowing, and aggressive automation. The account should be useful even when a reader never clicks a Hardware Radar link.

## Interpretation safeguards

Early evidence is noisy. Do not aggressively optimize from one day, one post, a handful of visitors, one query, one retailer click, or operator QA. Distinguish `OPERATOR/QA ACTIVITY` from `AUDIENCE SIGNAL` where practical and allow enough observation time to identify sustained patterns.

Do not prioritize solely by follower count, raw page views, article count, product count, posting volume, or search impressions without context. Prefer evidence of useful discovery, meaningful engagement, buying intent, repeat value, and commercial relevance.

Search Console and GA4 describe different parts of a basic, incomplete funnel:

```text
SEARCH/SOCIAL DISCOVERY
→ HARDWARE RADAR LANDING
→ CONTENT/PRODUCT/TOOL ENGAGEMENT
→ RETAILER REFERRAL
```

No downstream purchase or conversion may be claimed without separate evidence.

## Weekly decision questions

The review asks:

1. How are people discovering Hardware Radar?
2. Which pages and topics attract attention and useful engagement?
3. Which products receive interest?
4. Are visitors using the catalog and comparison?
5. Are visitors following retailer destinations, and for which products or retailers?
6. Which social topics generate meaningful engagement?
7. What appears weak, uncertain, or little used?
8. What should expand next—and what should not be built yet?

Examples are directional, not automatic rules: growing compatibility queries may justify investigating compatibility content or tools; sustained 16GB-versus-32GB interest may justify capacity content; sustained catalog use may justify broader Atlas coverage; product-page interest may justify more governed destinations; growing referrals may justify destination coverage and commercial evidence preservation; weak comparison use argues against speculative polish; value-DDR5 interest may justify bounded coverage; and demonstrated storage demand may inform category expansion.

## Compact output

```text
PERIOD

Search:
- impressions, clicks, notable queries and landing pages

On-site:
- notable landing, content, product, catalog, and comparison behavior

Retailer referrals:
- outbound clicks and notable products or retailer domains

Social:
- activity and meaningful engagement or topics

What changed:
- strongest emerging signals
- weak or uncertain signals

Decision:
- CONTINUE | INVESTIGATE | EXPAND | DEFER
- one or a small number of evidence-supported priorities
```

No elaborate reporting infrastructure is required.

## Beacon and future product direction

GA4 provides near-term operational web and referral analytics. Beacon remains the future governed first-party product/business-intelligence layer and should not be connected merely to duplicate GA4. Canonical dimensions such as `destinationId`, `atlasProductId`, `retailerId`, `marketplace`, and `sourceSurface` belong there only when the additional intelligence is justified.

Audience evidence may eventually establish demand for the documented RAM Market Overview or Hardware Radar Market Terminal, but visual appeal or social enthusiasm does not authorize either. **DENSITY IS EARNED BY DATA.**

## Credibility dossier

Affiliate reapplication and partnership timing should follow accumulated credibility rather than an arbitrary date or single traffic threshold:

- **Site:** substantive content, useful tools, growing catalog, product pages, and retailer destinations.
- **Audience:** search impressions and clicks, users, repeat usage where measurable, and an active social presence.
- **Commercial intent:** product-page engagement, comparison usage, and outbound retailer referrals.
- **Brand:** active official profiles, consistent useful publishing, and recognizable Hardware Radar identity.

## Current phase

The current operating priority is **AUDIENCE & AUTHORITY**: useful distribution, social presence, SEO observation, content authority, selective product and retailer coverage, and measurement. Infrastructure-heavy work should resume only for a demonstrated product, evidence, safety, or operational need.
