# IC-FORGE-TRUSTED-OPERATOR-SELF-SERVICE-COMPLETION-P2

Forge's loopback-only trusted operator runtime composes the existing Atlas catalog administrator, Mercury production destination owner, manual-current-price affiliate workbook, bounded link verifier, and durable operator audit. It creates no canonical subsystem.

The runtime supports registered-manufacturer selection or evidence-backed Atlas registration, DRAFT RAM product creation, permitted product edits, governed retailer destinations, exact-product link checks, and Newegg affiliate add/replace/disable/history. Affiliate writes preserve the workbook schema and unrelated entries, retain a digest-addressed prior copy, serialize writers, revalidate the workbook digest immediately before atomic replacement, and remain independent of market ranking, eligibility, Current, History, Picks, and Cheapest.

Product Manager is rebuilt from canonical owners on each trusted local request, so successful mutations become visible without a manual export. Manual projection import remains diagnostic fallback. Search, filters, and selected-product context remain browser state.

## DRAFT correction and destination workflow amendment

Broad product-name, classification, capacity/module, physical, compatibility, and supported specification corrections are authorized only for an unbound `DRAFT` / `PENDING` record. Atlas product ID, manufacturer, MPN, governance, provenance, revision order, schema, duplicate detection, and capacity equality remain invariant. Existing destinations make a broad correction ineligible; ACTIVE products retain the narrower established edit scope.

Retailer choice is projected from Atlas's active registered-retailer owner. Forge extracts Amazon ASIN or Newegg commerce identities from an exact HTTPS product page, while Mercury remains responsible for URL canonicalization, exact binding, replay, collision, supersession, serialized/atomic retention, and audit ordering. For Newegg, `/p/<id>` is retained as product-page identity and an explicit case-insensitive `Item=<id>` is retained separately as the offer/listing identity used by established Mercury acquisition owners. They are not required to match. Known tracking parameters alone may be removed; duplicates, malformed Item values, and unsupported query semantics fail closed. Path-only legacy records remain readable and are never silently assigned an Item identity. A DRAFT destination is retained for operator workflow but remains explicitly non-public and grants no market or publication authority.

An eligible `DRAFT` / `PENDING` product may append exactly one validated HTTPS manufacturer evidence source through the existing Atlas edit owner. The prior evidence array must remain an exact prefix, the new source ID must be unique, and operator identity, revision, single-writer lock, schema validation, and atomic product-file replacement remain mandatory. Product ID, manufacturer, brand, MPN, governance, and unrelated provenance cannot change through this amendment. ACTIVE records remain ineligible. A bound DRAFT may correct evidence, but the result explicitly requires operator revalidation of its existing destination; no destination is silently changed or retired.

Product creation remains DRAFT. No generic canonical Atlas readiness/activation owner exists for this runtime, so Forge exposes no activation control. Link reachability never establishes exact-product identity and never retires a destination automatically.

All certification writes use disposable fixture copies. Production Atlas, destinations, workbook, Current, History, publication, release, and deployment remain outside fixture certification.

### New-product affiliate workbook admission

The manual-current-price affiliate workbook remains the sole affiliate evidence owner. An authenticated ADD for a product absent from that workbook may append one row only when Atlas resolves the exact product as `ACTIVE / READY` and Mercury resolves exactly one active, complete Newegg destination. The admission row derives identity, specifications, and destination references from those canonical owners; price, shipping, seller, availability, Amazon evidence, Current authority, and import authority remain blank, unknown, or `NO` as appropriate. No operator-entered market fact is synthesized.

Admission executes under the existing single-writer lock and expected-workbook digest, preserves all preceding rows and unrelated ZIP entries, extends the established worksheet/table/validation ranges, preserves workbook styles and formulas, writes through a same-directory temporary file, validates the result, retains the digest-addressed predecessor, and atomically replaces the workbook. Duplicate product rows, duplicate affiliate URL ownership, stale digests, lifecycle drift, missing or ambiguous destinations, malformed workbooks, and concurrent mutation fail closed. Forge's existing operator audit remains the durable action receipt; no parallel affiliate store or authority is introduced.

Affiliate merchant identity follows the canonical Newegg dual-identifier contract. A valid case-insensitive `Item` query value is the listing identity when present; otherwise the `/p/<id>` path identity applies. The affiliate URL must resolve exactly to the governed destination listing and cannot be used as identity evidence or as an implicit destination correction. Add, replace, and disable alter routing only and remain independent from price qualification, Current, History, Cheapest, Picks, recommendations, publication, release, and deployment.
## Governed DRAFT identity correction

`CORRECT_DRAFT_IDENTITY` is an authenticated Atlas mutation, not an ordinary edit. It is limited to revision-current `DRAFT` / `PENDING` products and accepts only the corrected MPN plus validated manufacturer evidence. Atlas derives the product ID, enforces repository uniqueness, preserves prior MPN evidence, and records old/new identity lineage in the operator audit. Current, History, publication, effective retailer destinations, or other durable acquisition identity dependencies block execution. Destination records are immutable evidence and are never rewritten as a side effect. Resolving such a dependency requires its existing governed owner before the identity correction can be retried.
# Coordinated DRAFT identity/destination correction

An eligible `DRAFT / PENDING` product identity correction may coordinate Atlas and Mercury destination persistence through a narrow durable intent owned by the trusted Forge operation. It is not a third catalog or destination owner.

The certified phases are `PREPARED`, `ATLAS_CORRECTED`, and `COMPLETED`. Atlas replacement is followed by one locked, digest-revalidated Mercury append that retires each old effective destination and creates its corrected-identity successor. Original destination records and evidence remain immutable. Retired historical destination records may reference an Atlas identity that has since been replaced; they never become effective or public.

Current/History dependencies, released identities, stale revisions, destination drift, collisions, malformed state, and concurrent mutation fail closed. Exact replay must not append another destination record or operator audit. A process interruption after either persistence boundary must resume from durable intent.
