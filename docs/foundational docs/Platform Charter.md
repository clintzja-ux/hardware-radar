Hardware Intelligence Platform Charter
Mission

Build the world's most trusted, extensible hardware intelligence platform by combining structured product knowledge, verified market data, deterministic validation, and AI-assisted reasoning into a reusable foundation for applications, APIs, and future services.

Vision

Rather than becoming another hardware review website, the platform exists to understand computer hardware.

Every recommendation should be:

Explainable
Verifiable
Reproducible
Data-driven
Continuously improving
Core Principles
Truth over clicks

Never recommend a product simply because it pays more.

Validation before publication

Nothing reaches users without passing Sentinel.

Knowledge before presentation

Atlas is the source of truth.

The website is merely a presentation layer.

Platform first

Applications come and go.

The platform endures.

AI should reason, not hallucinate

Aurora should never invent hardware facts.

It reasons only from validated platform knowledge.

Extensibility by design

Every new hardware category should plug into the platform instead of requiring redesign.

One more subsystem I'd like to introduce

We've talked about Atlas, Mercury, Sentinel, Forge, Aurora, Beacon, and Gateway.

I'd add one more:

Observatory

Purpose:

Learn from the outside world.

Responsibilities could include:

retailer monitoring
manufacturer announcements
specification revisions
BIOS updates
firmware releases
product launches
recalls
end-of-life notices
market events

Mercury answers:

"What are prices doing?"

Observatory answers:

"What changed in the hardware world?"

That separation keeps market data distinct from ecosystem intelligence.
