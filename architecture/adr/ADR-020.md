ADR-020 — First-Party Data Is a Strategic Platform Asset

With principles:

Hardware Radar distinguishes external licensed data, first-party observations, and derived intelligence.

First-party data is owned and governed independently of retailer-source data, subject to privacy, consent and applicable legal requirements.

All derived intelligence preserves provenance sufficient to determine the rights of its underlying inputs.

Beacon is the canonical owner of platform behavioral and demand intelligence.

Mercury does not become a general-purpose analytics warehouse.

Hardware Radar practices data minimization and does not collect identifiable user information merely because it can.

And I would add one more principle that I think will matter tremendously later:

Never contaminate first-party datasets with licensed retailer content in a way that makes their provenance inseparable.

For example, don't create one mysterious table:

analytics_data

containing everything.

Maintain boundaries:

Atlas       WHAT IS IT?

Mercury     WHAT DOES THE MARKET OFFER?

Beacon      WHAT IS HAPPENING ON HARDWARE RADAR?

Compass     WHAT SHOULD THE BUYER DO?

Aurora      HOW DO WE EXPLAIN IT?

That's clean.
