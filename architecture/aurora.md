Aurora becomes explainable AI

One thing I want us to preserve from day one is explainability.

Aurora shouldn't say:

Buy Kit A.

It should say:

I recommend Kit A because:

Atlas shows it meets your capacity and compatibility requirements.
Mercury indicates it's 12% below its 90-day average price.
Sentinel has verified its specifications.
Forge generated this comparison using the latest validated data.

Every recommendation should be traceable back through the platform.
