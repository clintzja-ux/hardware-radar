Methodology

Current values use one qualified item price per product, so products with more retailers receive no extra statistical weight. Prices exclude applicable shipping, taxes, and fees. Current prices are governed independently from the historical series.

Historical movement compares the latest and previous distinct-time standalone-comparable observations; the table displays both comparators. The comparable history range and “Latest at observed low” badge also describe that historical series, not the independent Current price. “Lowest Hardware Radar-observed price” means the lowest qualifying observation since tracking began—not an all-time or internet-wide low. Two distinct comparable timestamps are required for movement and observed-low status. Median and current qualified range are suppressed until a lens has at least five currently priced products.
