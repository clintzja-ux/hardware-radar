How We Choose

Every displayed comparison follows governed eligibility and transparency rules.

Understanding the monitored scope and price basis is as important as the listed price.

Our Comparison Process

We Start With Price

We evaluate listed prices from the products and retailers currently monitored by Hardware Radar.

Prices change over time. We show an observation only while it meets our publication, freshness, and confidence requirements.

We Verify the Product

Price alone is not enough.

We verify the product specifications to make sure you are comparing the correct memory type, capacity, speed, and retailer listing.

We Compare Qualifying Value

Hardware Radar starts with the lowest qualifying listed price within its monitored coverage.

A listed price may not include shipping, tax, or other mandatory charges. We identify the price basis and leave unknown charges unknown.

We Show Alternatives

Monitored coverage is not the whole market.

When multiple comparable offers qualify, Hardware Radar can show a small set of alternatives. If coverage is insufficient, no substitute or estimated offer is shown.

Comparison Eligibility Is Earned

Offers qualify for comparison through:

  • Governed listed-price observations
  • Value
  • Suitability

Rankings are not sold to retailers or advertisers. Affiliate relationships do not determine offer ordering.

Transparency builds trust. That is why we explain how every qualifying comparison is formed.
